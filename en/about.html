关于传道 AI：以代码为墨，以 AI 为锋，赋能企业数字化新生

自 2019 年成立以来，传道 AI 始终锚定 "用技术破解业务痛点，用 AI 激活增长潜力" 的核心定位，从专注软件开发的初创团队，逐步成长为覆盖全国 18 个核心城市、服务超 300 家企业客户的数字化解决方案服务商。我们深知，在 AI 与产业深度融合的时代，"好的软件" 不仅是代码的集合，更是贴合业务逻辑、承载战略目标的 "数字引擎"，因此五年来始终以 "技术深耕 + 需求洞察" 双轮驱动，在软件开发领域持续突破，助力企业跨越数字化转型的 "最后一公里"。

Technical Strength and Team Building

In terms of technical strength and team building, Chuandao AI has built a core team of "practical + innovative" members, with more than 120 current members, of which technical R&D personnel account for more than 70%, and 85% have more than 6 years of software development experience. The core team members are all from major Internet companies and leading technology companies, and have led the development of projects with tens of millions of users such as financial risk control systems, intelligent manufacturing management platforms, and new retail data middle platforms. For example, Technical Director Zhang led the team to complete the development of an AI quality inspection system for a listed car company, improving product detection efficiency by 60% and reducing misjudgment rate to below 0.3%. We always adhere to the concept of "technology feeding back business", set up special R&D funds, and invest 20% of revenue annually in AI algorithm optimization, low-code platform iteration, and industry solution innovation. We have independently developed three core technology products: "Chuandao Low-Code Development Platform", "AI Data Cleaning Tool", and "Intelligent Business Process Engine", with a total of 42 software copyrights and 6 invention patents, and our technical strength has passed dual certification of ISO9001 quality management system and CMMI3 software capability maturity model.

Core Business and Service System

In terms of core business and service system, Chuandao AI focuses on the main line of "software development" and has formed three major business segments: "customized development + productized solutions + full-cycle operation and maintenance", covering digital needs in multiple industries and scenarios.

Customized Software Development

For enterprise personalized needs, we provide full-process services from requirement research, architecture design, code development to testing and launch, covering enterprise management systems (ERP/CRM/OA), industry-specific software (medical data management platform, education smart classroom system, manufacturing MES system), and mobile applications (APP / Mini Program / H5) in three major directions. For example, the "Smart Teaching Management Platform" developed for a chain education institution integrates four major modules: course booking, learning analysis, home-school interaction, and academic statistics. After launch, it helped the institution improve the efficiency of academic staff by 45% and increase parent satisfaction from 82% to 96%.

AI-Powered Solutions

Deeply integrating AI technology with industry scenarios, we develop software systems with intelligent decision-making and automated processing capabilities, such as the "AI Risk Control Review System" created for financial industry customers, which uses machine learning algorithms to analyze user credit data in real-time, reducing review efficiency from manual 1 hour/case to 3 minutes/case, and reducing bad debt rate by 18%; the "AI Smart Recommendation System" developed for retail enterprises, based on user consumption behavior profiles to achieve precise product push, helping customers increase average order value by 25% and repurchase rate by 30%.

Full-Cycle Technical Operation and Maintenance Services

Breaking the traditional model of "development as the end point", we provide customers with 1-3 years of exclusive operation and maintenance services, including system bug fixes, feature iterations, performance optimization, data security assurance, etc., equipped with a 7×24 hour technical support team with an average response time of no more than 30 minutes, ensuring long-term stable operation of software systems. By the end of 2024, we have served more than 300 customers in 12 industries including education, healthcare, finance, manufacturing, and retail, with a 100% project delivery rate and 68% customer repurchase rate. Among them, the cooperation with a medical device company has lasted for 4 years, gradually expanding from the initial equipment data management software development to AI remote diagnosis assistance system construction, becoming a "long-term partner" for customer digital transformation.
